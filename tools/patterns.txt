47. Heirloom Afghan  (Everything together)
Repeat: multiple of 12 sts + 4, over 8 rows. A gallery piece. 1600 stitches.
Stitch pattern: Sampler afghan: garter border, cable panels (C4F and C4B), a Fair Isle diamond band, lace diamonds (yo with ssk and k2tog) and a bobble band, separated by garter ridges.
Yarn: A Cream wool, B Cranberry wool, C Sage wool, D Honey wool.
Size: 40 sts × 40 rows, scaled down for the game.
Note: On the Fair Isle and bobble bands use a separate small ball of A for each side border and twist the yarns at the color change; do not carry A across the band. Strand only B and C inside the Fair Isle band.
Cast on 40 sts in A.
Rows 1–2: with A, k to end
Row 3 (RS): with A, k2, *p1, k4, p1; rep from * to last 2 sts, k2
Row 4 (WS): with A, k3, *p4, k2; rep from * to last st, k1
